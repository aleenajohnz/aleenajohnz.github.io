1. Project Overview

This project analyses weekly employee performance within a content review operation. The analysis focuses on attendance, productivity, quality, accuracy and target achievement.


The project is based on a synthetic dataset inspired by the type of weekly reporting performed in an enterprise content review environment.

2. Business Problem

Management needs a clear view of employee attendance and performance to identify trends, monitor targets and identify employees or teams that may require additional support or coaching.

Key questions:
  • What is the weekly attendance rate?
  • Which teams have the highest productivity?
  • How many employees achieved their targets?
  • Which employees have lower performance?
  • Does high productivity correspond with high quality?
  • Which weeks have the highest absence?

3. Dataset

The dataset contains synthetic weekly employee-level records representing a content review operation.


  • Employee ID
  • Team
  • Reporting Week
  • Working Days
  • Present Days
  • Absent Days
  • Leave Days
  • Cases Reviewed
  • Target Cases
  • Productivity %
  • Quality %
  • Accuracy %
  • Errors
  • Adherence %
  • Overall Performance Score

4. Data Cleaning

The raw dataset will be checked and cleaned before analysis.

  • Identify duplicate employee records.
  • Check missing values.
  • Validate dates and weekly reporting periods.
  • Check numerical fields for invalid values.
  • Validate attendance calculations.
  • Check productivity calculations.
  • Standardise team and performance categories.

  • Productivity by Team

  • Performance Status

9. Key Insights

Findings will be added after completing the analysis and dashboard.

The findings will be based on the calculated results from the dataset rather than assumed figures.

10. Business Recommendations

  • Monitor weekly attendance trends.
  • Identify employees consistently below target.
  • Compare productivity with quality before making performance decisions.
  • Provide targeted coaching where performance gaps are identified.
  • Use weekly dashboards to support management decisions.
